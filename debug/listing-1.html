Description

Sherry FitzGerald are delighted to present No. 1 Del Val Court, a unique and beautifully presented extended 3 bed, 1.5 bath semi-detached home in a quiet cul-de-sac. Modern open-plan extension to the rear with high-end finish, presented in excellent condition throughout, this impressive property offers bright, well-proportioned accommodation designed to meet the needs of modern family living, all within a highly convenient and sought-after coastal setting. The accommodation is thoughtfully laid out and briefly comprises of an inviting entrance hall, living room, extended open-plan kitchen/dining room, and guest WC on the ground floor. The impressive kitchen extension, completed in 2017, creates a bright and contemporary space. Upstairs, there are three well-proportioned bedrooms and a full family bathroom. Outside, the property benefits from a front garden with off-street parking and gated side access, while to the rear there is a private, landscaped garden. The location of this home is wonderful, set in a quiet cul-de-sac. It is a great area with all amenities on your doorstep including the coastal walk and cycleway, schools, shops, bus and DART at nearby Bayside. Viewing is highly recommended to appreciate this fine family home.

Hall - 1.8m x 4.8m Welcoming entrance hall featuring laminate effect timber flooring, decorative coving, a composite front door, alarm control panel, and practical understairs storage. Living Room - 3.25m x 4.6m Spacious living room featuring laminate effect timber flooring, decorative ceiling coving, a TV point, and an inset cast iron gas stove. Double doors open through to the kitchen. Kitchen/Dining Room - 9.0m x 3.15m An open-plan extended kitchen/dining featuring a modern fitted Kube kitchen which is complemented by a large island with Miller Brothers Carrara Silestone countertops and a range of integrated appliances including a De Dietrich double oven, Siemens five-ring gas hob, and Siemens dishwasher. Water softening and Filtered water tap installed. The room features laminate effect timber flooring, recessed lighting, and a large skylight. Sliding doors provide access to the utility area which is plumbed for a washing machine, while sliding glass doors provide direct access to the rear garden. Guest WC - 0.75m x 1.5m Guest WC featuring laminate effect timber flooring, a WC, and a wash hand basin with integrated storage cabinet. First Floor - Landing - 2.3m x 3.0m Hot press and access to the attic space. Bedroom 1 - 3.1m x 4.1m Spacious front-facing bedroom featuring fitted wardrobes, carpet flooring, a TV point, and blackout blinds Bedroom 2 - 2.85m x 3.75m Rear-facing bedroom featuring built-in wardrobes, a TV point and finished with carpet flooring. Bedroom 3 - 2.0m x 3.0m Front-facing bedroom featuring fitted wardrobes and carpet flooring. Bathroom - 2.25m x 2.5m Fully tiled bathroom featuring a WC, wash hand basin with integrated storage, a full bath, and a separate walk-in rain shower, a heated towel rail, extractor fan and recessed lighting. Outside - Front Garden - Walled front garden featuring a well-maintained lawn and landscaped flower beds. A driveway provides off-street parking for two cars, while a gated side entrance offers convenient access to the rear garden. Rear Garden - Private, walled rear garden with a lawn, paved patio area, and raised flower beds. Additional features include outdoor lighting and an external tap.
