Description

We are delighted to introduce No. 1 Cairnbrook Way to the market, a beautifully presented three-bedroom, three-bathroom end-of-terrace home, ideally positioned within the highly regarded Cairnbrook development. Arranged over three spacious levels, this well-maintained home offers bright, versatile accommodation with a private rear garden, balcony and excellent storage throughout. With its attractive end position, low-maintenance garden and well-planned accommodation, No. 1 Cairnbrook Way is a superb home that combines comfort, practicality and privacy in a sought-after Dublin 18 setting. The property has been carefully maintained and thoughtfully updated, with a calm neutral colour palette, timber-effect flooring and extensive fitted wardrobe storage adding to its appeal. The accommodation is particularly well proportioned, with the entrance level providing an impressive living room, defined dining area, contemporary kitchen and guest WC. French doors from the dining area open directly onto the rear patio and garden, creating a natural connection between the living and outdoor spaces. The first floor comprises two generous double bedrooms and a family bathroom, with Bedroom 2 further benefiting from access to a private balcony overlooking the development. The top floor is dedicated to the principal bedroom, which enjoys excellent privacy along with its own bathroom and useful dedicated storage. Cairnbrook is a highly regarded residential development of quality homes, discreetly positioned off Glenamuck Road South in Carrickmines. The location offers an excellent balance of convenience and tranquillity, with Foxrock, Stepaside and Kilternan all within easy reach, providing a superb selection of cafés, restaurants, specialist shops and everyday amenities. Carrickmines Retail Park is also nearby, while the surrounding area offers an abundance of sporting and leisure facilities. The location is exceptionally well served by transport links, with the L26 and L27 bus routes stopping directly at the entrance to the development. The LUAS, M50 and N11 are also within easy reach, offering excellent connectivity throughout Dublin and beyond. A number of excellent schools, recreational facilities and employment centres are also located nearby.
