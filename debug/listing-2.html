Description

***To bid on this property, please register on mysherryfitz.ie*** Sherry FitzGerald Brady O'Flaherty are pleased to introduce 1 Dillons Row, Maynooth to the market. Occupying a fantastic location, Dillons Row is a mature and well-respected development in a superb town centre position in the heart of this bustling university town. This substantial 'A-rated' 6-bedroom end-of-terrace home offers a rare opportunity to acquire a property of impressive scale and flexibility, which has been extensively upgraded and extended to a very high standard in recent years. Ideally positioned close to all amenities, this home will appeal to a wide range of buyers seeking space, versatility and energy efficiency. Maynooth boasts an enviable range of amenities including excellent transport to the city, University Campus, myriad of shopping, schools, pubs & trendy eateries. The nearby magnificent Carton Avenue also provides a superb amenity for all the family to enjoy and leads one into the expansive grounds of Carton House & Demesne. Extending to a generous approx. 146 sqm (1,570 sq.ft), this property is impeccably well presented throughout, offering ample bedroom and living spaces. Accommodation includes; welcoming hallway, living room, expansive open plan living / kitchen / dining room, downstairs ensuite bedroom, while on the first floor there 5 fine sized bedrooms, shower room & main bathroom. Ample, paved off street parking to front and lovely private, low maintenance enclosed rear garden. All in all, a most wonderfully located, energy efficient home in the heart of this expanding university town.
